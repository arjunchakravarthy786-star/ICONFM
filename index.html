About the Department

The Department of Chemistry at IIT Patna focuses on cutting-edge research across organic, materials, and physical chemistry. We foster interdisciplinary work and industry-academia collaboration to address applied problems in energy and light-based materials.

Our Mission

This conference is a Collaboration Platform: speakers **present unsolved research problems and bottlenecks** (not just success stories). Listeners register as Problem Solvers, propose solutions, and form collaborations. The goal: accelerate actionable solutions and cross-disciplinary teams.
