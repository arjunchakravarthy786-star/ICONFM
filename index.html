About Our Institution

The Indian Institute of Technology Patna (IIT Patna) is one of the premier technical institutes of India, established to foster excellence in education, research, and innovation. Located in the vibrant Bihta campus, IIT Patna offers cutting-edge programs in engineering, sciences, and humanities while nurturing a culture of interdisciplinary learning. The institute is committed to advancing frontier technologies, promoting industry–academia collaboration, and contributing to national development through impactful research and skilled human resource development.

About the Department

The Department of Chemistry at IIT Patna focuses on cutting-edge research across organic, materials, and physical chemistry. We foster interdisciplinary work and industry-academia collaboration to address applied problems in energy and light-based materials.

Our Mission

This conference is a Collaboration Platform: speakers present unsolved research problems and bottlenecks (not just success stories). Listeners register as Problem Solvers, propose solutions, and form collaborations. The goal is to accelerate actionable solutions and cross-disciplinary teams.
